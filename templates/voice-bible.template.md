# [Name]: Voice Bible

A reference for writing in [Name]'s voice. Give it to an AI before it drafts or edits anything for [Name].

**Read in this order.** The test, then "Hard no's" and "Leave these alone," then everything else. Those first sections are where a default AI draft goes wrong.

**Built from:** [what went in, for example: a 25-minute interview, 6 writing samples (about 4,000 words), 3 edit pairs]
**Evidence level:** [Starter / Solid / Strong]
**Weakest point:** [one line, for example: "the finished samples were drafted with AI help" or "only one edit pair"]
**Confirmed with [Name]:** [yes / no, built on the evidence alone]
**Last refreshed:** [date]

When this document and [Name]'s real writing disagree, the writing wins. Update the rule.

**Evidence tags.** Every rule carries one.

- `[strong]` seen 5 or more times, or in every piece out of three or more for a once-per-piece habit, or made 2 or more times in [Name]'s edits
- `[moderate]` seen 2 to 4 times, or removed once in an edit and never seen in [Name]'s typed writing
- `[said]` [Name] stated it
- `[absent]` it never appears in [Name]'s typed writing, with the sample size given
- Anything seen only once lives under "Unconfirmed" at the bottom until more evidence shows up.

For rules about sentence length, punctuation and formatting, only typed sightings count. For how [Name] tells stories, argues and jokes, spoken ones count too.

## The test

Before anything goes out in [Name]'s voice, ask: **would [Name] actually say this out loud, to a real person?**

[One or two lines that make the test specific to this person. Example: "Not 'would a consultant say this.' Would [Name] say it to a coworker over lunch."]
