## The principles

1. **Evidence over taste.** Every rule in the bible comes from something in my material. You are describing how I write. Your own preferences about good writing do not come into it.
2. **My exact words.** Quote me word for word, with my spelling and punctuation. Never tidy a quote and never invent one. An invented quote attributed to me is the worst error you can make here. A typo is not a habit, so do not build a rule on one.
3. **No inherited bans.** The catalog of common AI tells below is a set of questions to ask about my writing. If I do one of those things naturally, it is mine. The same goes for turns of phrase that come from a first language other than English. They are part of my voice and belong under "Leave these alone," unless I said I want them smoothed out.
4. **Thin evidence means a short bible.** Leave out any section you cannot support. A short true bible beats a long guessed one.
5. **Spoken is not written.** Dictated material runs longer and looser than my writing, and its punctuation belongs to the transcription software. 6. **Rules get performed.** An AI that learns I like some move will use it in every paragraph. Anything I do only now and then needs a ceiling.
7. **Some evidence outranks other evidence.** When two sources disagree, go with the one higher on this list, and tell me about the disagreement at the check-in.

## Sentence shapes

### `not-x-but-y` Negate, then restate
Denying something nobody claimed in order to deliver the "real" answer.
> "It's not just a budget. It's a statement of values."

Countable.

Countable.

Countable.

Countable.

Countable.

### `hedge-stack` Stacked hedges
> "This can often help to potentially reduce costs."

Countable.

### `even-rhythm` Every sentence the same medium length

Countable.

## What an AI does to a writer's material

These four are different. You cannot find them by reading someone's own writing, because they describe what happens when an AI works on it. They do not go into piles. They show up in a person's edits to AI drafts, and they belong in a bible when those edits show them.

### `invented-specifics` Details the writer never supplied

A blunt opinion softened, a real disagreement balanced into "both have merit," a firm decision rewritten as "worth considering."

Judgment call.
