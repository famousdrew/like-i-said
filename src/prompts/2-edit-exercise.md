We are going to run an edit exercise. You will write a few short passages in your own default style, and I will rewrite them the way I would actually say them. The differences between your version and mine are the best evidence there is of how my voice differs from yours.

## Your rules

1. If my interview is in this chat, use it for material. If it is not, ask me for three topics I know well, with two or three real facts or opinions about each, and wait for my answer.
2. Write three passages of 120 to 180 words each, one at a time:
   - Passage 1 retells one of my stories.
   - Passage 2 argues one of my opinions.
   - Passage 3 explains something or gives advice in an area I know.
3. Write them the way you normally write. Do not imitate me. A polished, generic version is exactly what we need, because my edits will show what I change about it.
4. Use only facts I gave you. Do not invent names, numbers, quotes or events. If you need a detail I did not give, leave a gap like [detail?].
5. After each passage, say only this: "Rewrite this the way you would say it. Change anything, cut anything, or start over. Don't be polite. Paste your version when you're done." Then wait.
6. When I paste my version, do not comment on it, praise it or revise it. Ask one question: "Is there anything you changed that you can put into words? Anything in your version that still doesn't sound like you?" Take my answer, or "no," and move to the next passage.
7. If I say a passage got a fact wrong, note the correction and carry on.

## Closing step

After the third passage, give me everything in one code block so I can copy it, in this format:

```
# Edit pairs

## Pair 1: [topic]

### AI draft
[your passage, exactly as you wrote it]

### My version
[my rewrite, exactly as I pasted it]

### My comments
[what I said about my changes, word for word, or "none"]

### Fact corrections
[any, or "none"]
```

Copy my versions exactly. Do not fix anything in them.

Then tell me to save it as `edits.md`. Tell me the next step is the build prompt, which works best in a fresh chat with my interview, my edits and any writing samples pasted in.
